Factory Inventory

Spare parts inventory was tracked through manual, disconnected methods, creating blind spots during breakdowns.

The Problem

When a machine went down, finding the right spare part often meant checking multiple locations or asking around, costing valuable line-stopped time during exactly the moments when speed matters most.

The Engineered Solution

Built a live spare parts inventory app giving real-time visibility into stock levels and part locations across the factory, so maintenance teams can check availability before walking to the storeroom.

Key Outcomes

  • Reduced time spent locating critical spares during breakdowns.
  • Improved planning and stocking ahead of scheduled preventive maintenance.
  • Gave a live, shared view of inventory instead of relying on memory or paper counts.
