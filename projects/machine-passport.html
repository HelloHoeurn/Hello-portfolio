Machine Documents

Each machine's maintenance history, manuals, and specs were scattered across paper files with no single reference point.

The Problem

When a machine needed servicing or troubleshooting, tracing its full history — past repairs, manuals, spec sheets — meant digging through disconnected paper records, often with pages missing or out of date.

The Engineered Solution

Built a digital "passport" app: one record per physical machine, centralizing its documentation, service history, and specs behind a simple lookup so any technician can pull up an asset's full history on the spot.

Key Outcomes

  • Replaced paper trails with a live digital history per machine.
  • Gave maintenance and engineering teams instant access to specs and service records.
  • Created a durable documentation habit going forward, since every update lives in one app instead of a folder.
