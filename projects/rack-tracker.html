Pactics — Rack Tracker

Heavy-duty rack and storage infrastructure had no live system of record — location, status, and allocation lived in memory and paper notes.

The Problem

As storage racks and heavy-duty shelving were added, moved, and reallocated across the factory, there was no single place to check what existed, where it was, or what it was assigned to. Audits meant walking the floor.

The Engineered Solution

Designed and deployed a live web application to track every rack and storage asset in one place — current location, condition, and allocation history, accessible from any device on the floor.

Key Outcomes

  • Gave planning and floor teams a single source of truth for heavy-duty storage assets.
  • Cut down manual, walk-the-floor audits in favor of a live lookup.
  • Established a reusable app pattern now extended to other asset types across the Pactics ecosystem.
