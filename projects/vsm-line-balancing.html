VSM-led Line Balancing

A brief case study.

Context

Baseline takt 58s, WIP high between stations 3–4.

Diagnosis

Time studies and VSM revealed imbalance causing starvation and blocking.

Interventions

Rebalanced work elements, introduced standard work and work sequence.

Outcome

Lead time -28%, WIP -35%, throughput +12%.
