Industrial Floor Plan Optimization (PACTICS Layout)

Baseline takt was 58s with high WIP stacking between stations 3–4, causing repeated starvation and blocking downstream.

The Problem

Time studies and value stream mapping revealed a structural imbalance in work-element distribution across the line, which manifested as excess in-process inventory and inconsistent output rates near the middle of the flow.

The Engineered Solution

Fully re-engineered production flow layouts using AutoCAD Web, precision-placing heavy machinery and adjusting physical safety boundaries to stay within compliance limits. Work elements were rebalanced across stations and paired with standard work documentation and a defined work sequence.

Key Outcomes

  • Lead time reduced by 28%.
  • Work-in-process inventory reduced by 35%.
  • Throughput increased by 12%.
