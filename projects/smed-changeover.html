SMED Changeover Reduction

Average changeover time sat at 42 minutes, with the majority classified as internal (machine-stopped) work.

The Problem

Video analysis and spaghetti diagramming of operator movement during changeovers exposed significant wasted motion and steps being performed while the line was fully stopped that didn't need to be.

The Engineered Solution

Converted internal setup steps to external steps wherever possible, so preparation could happen while the line was still running. Introduced kitting for tooling and consumables, and swapped slow fasteners for quick-release clamps to cut manual adjustment time.

Key Outcomes

  • Changeover time reduced by 42%.
  • Line uptime improved by 7 percentage points.
  • Estimated annual savings of $90k.
