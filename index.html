About

I'm transitioning from IE Lean & Innovation Supervisor into a Lead Engineer role, bridging shop-floor systems engineering with data-driven software tooling. My work sits at the boundary between physical operations — layout design, line balancing, machine placement — and the digital systems that track them: ERP records, BOM accuracy, and live performance dashboards.

I care about closing the gap between what's happening on the floor and what the data says is happening — and building the tools that make that gap disappear.
